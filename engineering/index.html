Individual dissertation · Apr 2025

Designing a lighter Formula Student suspension upright

How far can mass be reduced without losing structural credibility or manufacturing practicality?

I developed the load model, material screen, CAD redesigns, mesh-convergence study, finite-element analyses and machining-cost comparison across six design iterations.

Iteration 5 offered the strongest practical balance: 18.43% lower modelled mass, a 4.80 safety factor and an estimated machining cost of £75.53.

Finite-element analysis · Individual project

When a finer model changes the answer

Testing 72 mesh configurations isolated the effects of element shape, order and density on a loaded spanner. Refinement captured 42.9% more peak stress; quadrilateral meshes achieved comparable displacement with approximately 40% fewer elements.

TakeawayThe mesh changes captured peak stress, while element choice also affects the computational cost of comparable displacement results.

Control systems · Laboratory project

Modelling and tuning a water-level control system

System identification, MATLAB/Simulink modelling and PID tests compared predicted and measured water-level response. The theoretical time constant was 127.2 seconds versus 138 seconds experimentally: an 8.4% difference.

Report↗PDF · 3.1 MB

TakeawayThe 127.2-second theoretical time constant and 138-second measured result show why a model needs experimental comparison.

Thermodynamic systems · Individual project

Selecting a working fluid for biomass CHP

Steam and organic Rankine cycles were compared, then n-pentane, HFE-7000 and HFE-7100 were assessed against operating constraints. Sourced fluid properties remain distinct from original calculations; the study does not claim one universally best fluid.

TakeawayFluid choice depends on the system requirements; sourced property data and calculated performance should remain traceable.

Experimental heat transfer · Group project

Testing convection and radiation theory

A four-person laboratory study compared theoretical and measured heat-transfer coefficients for a heated horizontal cylinder under natural and forced convection. Agreement improved as conditions intensified: the gap between theory and measurement fell from 50% to 1.8% as surface temperature rose, and from 16.8% to 8.0% as air velocity increased.

Report↗PDF · 2.3 MB

TakeawayThe team’s results show that agreement can vary with the operating condition; one aggregate error can hide that pattern.

Structural testing · Group project

Measuring off-axis bending in a cantilever beam

A four-person experiment measured horizontal and vertical tip deflection of a 515 mm beam (E = 170 GPa) loaded at 0°, 30° and 60° to its principal axes, with every test repeated three times and averaged. Deflections stayed linear-elastic and followed theory for on- and off-axis loading; a residual horizontal deflection at 0° was traced to off-centre weights.

Report↗PDF · 1.6 MB

TakeawayAgreement should be reported by direction and angle: the one discrepancy was a horizontal deflection that theory predicts as zero.

Life-cycle analysis · Individual project

Cigarettes versus disposable vapes

A cradle-to-grave comparison of a pack of Marlboro Gold and an Elf Bar 600 across environmental, economic and social indicators. The disposable vape carried about four times the climate impact (0.464 vs 0.118 kg CO₂e) and more than seven times the production cost (£0.95 vs £0.13), with its lithium-ion battery alone accounting for £0.87 of the materials bill.

Report↗PDF · 4.8 MB

TakeawayThe functional unit and unit conversions determine whether the headline comparison is meaningful.

Infrastructure modelling · Individual project

Renewable power for Dominica

Design of a 60 MW combined-cycle plant for Dominica, an off-grid Caribbean island, burning locally produced landfill gas. The Brayton and Rankine cycles were sized from first principles (48.7 MW from the gas turbine, 11.5 MW from steam) to a combined thermal efficiency of 42%, with equipment selection and a 20-year cost-of-electricity estimate.

Report↗PDF · 3.5 MB

TakeawayGeneration economics depend on the relationship between energy demand, fuel supply and equipment performance.

A smaller heat exchanger – and its trade-offs

The model reduced area from 13.072 to 4.702 m² and tube count from 124 to 45, raising the heat-transfer coefficient from 200 to 404 W/m²K. The study examined how a more compact design changes thermal performance and flow requirements.

Refrigeration analysis · Individual project

Locating useful-energy losses in an HVAC cycle

First- and second-law analysis of an R134a vapour-compression cycle. Of 12.1 kW of exergy destroyed, the evaporator accounted for 9.1 kW and the compressor 2.5 kW; the cycle reached a COP of 4.25 against a reversible limit of 4.53, a second-law efficiency of 93.7%.

Report↗PDF · 4.7 MB

TakeawayEnergy and exergy analysis offer complementary views of cycle efficiency and the sources of useful-energy loss.
