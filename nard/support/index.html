Persian backgammon for iPhone

Found a problem, or have an idea for the game? Write to amir@amirpousti.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version if you are reporting a bug.

Questions

How do I move a checker?

Tap Roll, then tap a checker. Every point it can move to lights up; tap one of them. If a checker can use both dice, the combined destination lights up too, marked with the two numbers. When only one checker can move, it is selected for you.

How do I bear off?

Once all fifteen of your checkers are in your home board, tap a checker and then tap it again, or tap the tray in the frame on your side of the board. Checkers that have left the board stack up in the tray.

Can I take back a move?

Yes. The undo button (top left) takes back your last move, right up until the computer rolls.

What does the hint button do?

It draws the move the strongest computer level would play from your position. It is there to help you learn; use it as much as you like.

How do the computer levels differ?

Easy plays more or less at random. Medium makes real moves with regular mistakes. Hard plays a solid game. Very hard also looks ahead at every roll you could make next. The dice are the same plain random dice for both sides at every level.

Is there a doubling cube?

No. Nard follows takhteh nard as played at home in Iran: single games, or a match to 3, 5 or 7 points, where a gammon scores 2 and a backgammon 3.

Can I play against a friend?

Yes. Choose Two players and pass the phone. Online play is not available yet.

How do I change the board?

Open Settings (the gear) and choose from the five boards. The choice applies straight away, even mid-game.

My game disappeared.

An unfinished game is saved on your phone and appears as Resume on the home screen. Deleting the app deletes the saved game and your records, as nothing is stored anywhere else.

Does the app collect any data?

No. See the privacy policy.

Learning the game

If you have never played, open Learn from the home screen. Seven short lessons put you on the board with a set roll and ask for the move; by the end you know every rule.
