Privacy Policy for Nard

Effective 5 November 2026

The short version: Nard collects no data about you. Nothing leaves your phone.

What Nard is

Nard is a backgammon (takhteh nard) game for iPhone. It is free, contains no advertising, no purchases, no accounts and no subscriptions. It works entirely offline.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit or store any personal information. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or anything else, and it does not use analytics, tracking or advertising software of any kind.

What stays on your device

So that the game can pick up where you left off, the app keeps a small amount of information on your phone only:

  • your settings (board style, sound, point numbers)
  • an unfinished game, so you can resume it
  • your win and loss record against each computer level
  • which lessons you have completed

This information never leaves your device and we have no way of seeing it. Deleting the app deletes it.

Internet access

The app makes no network connections. Everything it needs is included in the download.

Third parties

Nard contains no third-party software development kits, advertising networks or analytics services, and shares nothing with anyone.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects none from children either. It is suitable for all ages.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Any future version of the app that collected data would say so clearly here and in the App Store listing.
