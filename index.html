About

From engineering to finance.

As a child, I wanted to understand how things worked. That curiosity led me to mechanical engineering at Queen Mary University of London, where I earned a First Class Honours degree.

Finance appealed to me for its opportunities and the real-world problems it tackles. I took that interest to UCL, alongside part-time analyst work in Dubai: underwriting acquisitions for a private real-estate portfolio and analysing pricing and margins for a textile import/export business.

The projects on this site bring those two sides together: company valuation, investment risk and engineering decisions tested through modelling and simulation.
