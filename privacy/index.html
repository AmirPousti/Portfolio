Privacy and analytics.

How I understand visits to this portfolio and the choices you have.

Optional Google Analytics

If you choose Accept in the cookie pop-up, Google Analytics uses cookies to measure visits, pages viewed and interactions such as outbound links and downloads. Its reports can include approximate countries and cities inferred from an internet connection, along with device and browser information. They do not tell me a visitor’s name or exact address.

The Google tag stays unloaded until you allow it. Declining leaves it off. Advertising storage, advertising personalisation and Google signals are disabled in this site’s configuration. No browser GPS access is requested.

Your choice is saved in this browser for up to 180 days. Use Cookie settings in the footer to change it at any time. Withdrawing permission disables further Google Analytics collection and removes this site’s Google Analytics cookies from your browser; it does not delete data already sent to Google. If your browser cannot save the choice, you may be asked again on the next page.

Read how Google uses information from sites that use its services↗ (opens in a new tab) and Google Analytics’ data collection information↗ (opens in a new tab).

Vercel Web Analytics

This website also uses Vercel’s cookie-free Web Analytics for aggregate visitor counts, page views, referrers, countries and device information. This runs separately from the optional Google Analytics choice. See Vercel’s explanation of the data it collects↗ (opens in a new tab).

Contact and external links

Email links open your own email application. LinkedIn and other external sites have their own privacy practices. For questions about this portfolio or its analytics, contact me.
