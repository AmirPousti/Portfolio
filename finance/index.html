Individual research project · Aug 2026

Ferrari: where scarcity drives value

Can an exceptional scarcity-led business justify an exceptional valuation?

I built a 24-sheet operating and valuation model covering Ferrari’s historical performance, forecast drivers, free cash flow, beta, WACC, peer multiples and dividends.

The original conclusion was deliberately restrained: the franchise was exceptional, but the blended target implied just 2.03% upside.

Macroeconomic research · Group project

Did credibility make disinflation less costly?

Inflation, expectations, unemployment and GDP were compared around New Zealand’s 1989–92 regime change. Inflation fell from 9.28% to 3.23%, but unemployment rose from 5.76% to 9.75%; concurrent reforms limited attribution to inflation targeting.

Report↗PDF · 2.4 MB

TakeawayFalling inflation alongside rising unemployment does not isolate the effect of inflation targeting when other reforms occur at the same time.
