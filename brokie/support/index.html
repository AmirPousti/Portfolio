Questions

How does a lesson work?

Each lesson opens with a short explainer card and a worked example, then five questions. Tap an answer, then Check. A wrong answer costs a heart and the question comes back at the end of the lesson until you get it right.

What are hearts?

You have five. Each wrong answer in a lesson or checkpoint costs one. A heart comes back every 30 minutes on its own, and finishing a practice session (Quick 5 or Review my mistakes) restores one straight away. Practice never costs hearts.

How do streaks work?

Finish at least one lesson, checkpoint or practice session in a day and the day counts. Miss a day and the streak resets, unless you have a streak freeze: you earn one each time you pass a course exam, and the app uses it automatically to cover a single missed day.

What is Daily?

The Daily tab builds a short ladder of lessons from the courses you have added, starting with the most basic course until it is finished. Starting any lesson from the Courses tab adds that course to Daily automatically. Your daily goal (5, 10 or 15 minutes) can be changed in Profile.

How do exams work?

Every course ends in an exam: twenty questions drawn from all of its lessons, no hearts and no second chances, with the explanation shown after each answer. 70% passes. You can pause an exam and resume it from the course page, and retake it as many times as you like. Passing earns a certificate, a badge and a streak freeze.

Why are some courses marked coming soon?

Five courses are live at launch: Basic Finance, Corporate Finance, Valuation and Financial Modelling, Capital Markets and Securities, and Economics and Central Banks. Investment Banking, Private Equity, Credit, Wealth and Fintech are being written and will arrive in updates.

How do I set a daily reminder?

Profile → Daily reminder. Choose a time and switch it on; iOS will ask once for permission to show notifications. Turn it off in the same place, or in iOS Settings → Notifications → Brokie.

My progress disappeared.

Progress is stored only on your phone. Deleting the app deletes it, and Reset progress in Profile clears it deliberately. Nothing is stored anywhere else, so it cannot be recovered from a server.

Can I use Brokie on two devices?

Not yet. There is no account, so progress does not sync between devices.

Is this financial advice?

No. Brokie teaches how finance works. It does not recommend what you should do with your money.

Does the app collect any data?

No. See the privacy policy.

Found an error in a lesson?

Please tell us. Lessons are written to be accurate at a level a finance master's student would recognise, and a correction from a reader is the fastest way to fix a slip.
