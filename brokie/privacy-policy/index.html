Privacy Policy for Brokie

Effective 5 November 2026

The short version: Brokie collects no data about you. Nothing leaves your phone.

What Brokie is

Brokie is a finance-learning app for iPhone: short lessons, quizzes and course exams, from money basics to valuation. It is free, contains no advertising, no purchases, no accounts and no subscriptions. It works entirely offline.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit or store any personal information. It does not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos or anything else, and it does not use analytics, tracking or advertising software of any kind.

What stays on your device

So that the app can pick up where you left off, it keeps a small amount of information on your phone only:

  • which lessons, checkpoints and exams you have completed, and your scores
  • your streak, XP, hearts and streak freezes
  • your settings: courses added to Daily, daily goal, appearance, reminder time
  • the questions you got wrong, so Practice can bring them back

This information never leaves your device and we have no way of seeing it. Reset progress in the Profile tab clears it; deleting the app deletes it.

Reminders

If you switch on a daily reminder, the app asks iOS to show one notification a day at the time you choose. The notification is scheduled on your phone by iOS itself; no server is involved and nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Sharing

If you choose to share a certificate or progress card, the app draws an image on your phone and hands it to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes from there is your choice; Brokie does not see or record it.

Internet access

The app makes no network connections. Lessons, fonts and images are all included in the download.

Third parties

Brokie contains no third-party software development kits, advertising networks or analytics services, and shares nothing with anyone.

Children

Because the app collects no data at all, it collects none from children either. It is an education app for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Any future version of the app that collected data (for example an optional account to sync progress between devices) would say so clearly here and in the App Store listing, and would ask for your consent where the law requires it.
